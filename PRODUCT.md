# Product

## Register

product

## Users

**Admin/Owner** — studio manager, operates the system multiple hours daily. Context: busy, high context-switching (schedule changes, sales entry, staff management), works in various lighting conditions (studio floor, office, mobile). Job: make the day run smoothly. Manages schedules, finances, staff, and client relationships.

**Trainer** — fitness/dance instructor. Uses the system for their own schedule, students, and payroll calculation. Context: often multi-tasking, checks before/after classes. Job: see their classes, track students, understand payment.

**Client** — studio member. Uses booking system and account dashboard on mobile primarily. Context: wants to self-manage subscription, schedule classes, see balance. Job: book a class, check remaining sessions, manage account.

## Product Purpose

SEKTA-CRM is a studio management system for fitness/dance studios. It centralizes scheduling, client management, attendance tracking, billing (deposits + session-based subscriptions), trainer payroll, and financial reconciliation. Success: the studio operates smoothly without paper, cash floats are tracked, trainers get paid correctly, and clients can self-serve booking and account management.

## Brand Personality

Professional, competent, no-nonsense. The studio is a business, not a lifestyle brand. The interface earns trust through accuracy and transparency, not through personality or delight. Serious about numbers and time (trainers depend on payroll, clients depend on booking accuracy).

Three words: **Professional, Competent, Transparent**.

## Anti-references

- **Gray, flat, corporate (late-1990s accounting software)** — sterile, soulless, discourages daily use.
- **Overloaded chaos (too many colors, competing elements, unclear hierarchy)** — confuses busy admin in a busy studio.
- **Dark gaming aesthetic (neons, depth, for gamers)** — this is a business tool, not entertainment.
- **Overly minimal (empty, cold, sparse)** — loses the functional substance that matters.

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity over aesthetics** — function is primary. A button must be obvious, data must be scannable. Decoration never competes with meaning.
2. **Calm + focus** — reduce visual noise. Admin needs to see one clear task at a time, not twenty options. Spare design, clean typography, breathing room.
3. **Speed of operation** — no extra clicks. Frequently-used operations surface without drilling. Information stays visible without endless scrolling.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- **WCAG AA minimum** (studio is accessible; no barriers).
- **Contrast 4.5:1 for body text** — critical. Admins are busy, lighting is variable. Older instructors and studio staff may have vision variations. Text must never strain.
- **Reduced motion support** — animations have fallback behavior for users with vestibular sensitivity.
